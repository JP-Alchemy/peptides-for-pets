What is a copper peptide?

The simple version first. The science is further down if you want it.

1

Peptides are tiny messengers.

Your body, and your animal’s body, is built from protein. Protein is made of long chains of building blocks called amino acids. A peptide is a very short chain, just a few links long. Short chains like this act like notes passed between cells, telling them what to do next.

2

A copper peptide carries a bit of copper.

One of those short chains is called GHK. It likes to hold on to a tiny bit of copper. Together they’re called GHK-Cu, or copper peptide. You’ll find it in plenty of skincare made for people.

Natural occurrence of GHK-Cu in animals.

3

Skin & Coat puts it on the skin.

We put it in a Spray Gel that goes straight onto the skin, every day. No tablets, no mixing into food.

4

It supports the skin’s own renewal.

Skin is always busy. Old cells wear away and new ones take their place. Underneath, skin makes collagen, the stretchy stuff that keeps it strong and smooth. Copper peptide is one of the signals that supports this natural renewal. It doesn’t do the work for the skin; it helps the skin do its own job. Healthy skin underneath means a healthy coat on top.
