Quick answers

·How long does a bottle last?

It depends on size. A 30 mL bottle lasts a small dog about 50 days and a dog over 30 kg about 21 days. A 50 mL horse bottle lasts an average horse about a month. The spray guide on the label and product page shows the exact figure.

·When will I see a difference?

Every animal is different, so we don’t promise a date. Use it daily for 30 days and take a photo on day 1, 10, 20 and 30, so you can judge for yourself.

·Why does it go in the fridge?

Copper peptides are delicate. Cold keeps an opened bottle at its best.
