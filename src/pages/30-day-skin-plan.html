What to look for along the way

Every animal is different, so we won’t promise a timeline. Each time you take a photo, check:

  • How the coat feels under your hand.
  • How it catches the light.
  • How the skin looks when you part the coat.
  • How much time they spend scratching or fussing at their coat.

When to stop

Skin & Coat is for everyday skin and coat care. If the skin gets worse, if the skin is broken, or if your animal seems unwell, stop using it and see your vet.
