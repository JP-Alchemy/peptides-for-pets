How It’s Made

Here’s where Skin & Coat is made, how, and what we won’t cut corners on.

Who makes it

A specialist compounding pharmacy lab

Skin & Coat is made by one of South Africa’s leading compounding pharmacies, in their specialist lab. This isn’t a shop counter. It’s a lab where pharmacists make products to a set formula, in batches, under strict records.

We don’t name our lab partner on the website. Ask us and we’ll send you their signed quality letter.

Facts that back “leading”: years compounding, SAPC registration, clean room standard, inspections or accreditation. We can state these without naming the pharmacy.

What “compounded” means

What “compounded” means

Pharmacists make it to a set formula, batch by batch, in a registered lab. Every batch is made under a Responsible Pharmacist, the person legally accountable for how it’s made.

Batch numbers

Every bottle has a batch number

Look on the label. That number tells us when your bottle was made and what went into it. If you ever have a question about your bottle, quote it and we’ll look it up.
