About the product

What is Skin & Coat?

A daily Spray Gel with copper peptide (GHK-Cu) that supports healthy skin and coat. It’s batch-made in a specialist compounding pharmacy lab in South Africa, for dogs, horses and cats.

What is a copper peptide?

A tiny protein messenger that carries a bit of copper. It supports the skin’s own natural renewal. People already use it in their own skincare. Read the full explainer.

How long does a bottle last?

It depends on size. A 30 mL bottle lasts a small dog about 50 days and a dog over 30 kg about 21 days. A 50 mL horse bottle lasts an average horse about a month. The spray guide on the label and product page shows the exact figure.

When will I see a difference?

Every animal is different, so we don’t promise a date. Use it daily for 30 days and take a photo on day 1, 10, 20 and 30, so you can judge for yourself.

Why does it go in the fridge?

Copper peptides are delicate. Cold keeps an opened bottle at its best.

Safety and use

Is it safe if they lick it?

With the pharmacist and a vet before publishing. Draft if confirmed: “Skin & Coat is made for use on the skin. A little licked off during grooming is [TO CONFIRM]. Try to let it dry first.”

Can I use it with shampoos and other coat products?

With the lab. Draft: “Yes. Apply it after bathing, once the coat is dry.”

Is it suitable for cats?

With the pharmacist and a vet. Cats groom far more than dogs and swallow what’s on their coat. Do not publish until confirmed.

Can I use it on puppies, kittens, foals, or pregnant animals?

With the pharmacist and a vet.

Can I use it on a horse that competes?

Competition rules. See the note on the horse product page.

What if the skin gets worse?

Stop using it and see your vet. Skin & Coat is for everyday skin and coat care.

What kind of product is it?

A topical Spray Gel for cosmetic and grooming use on animals.
