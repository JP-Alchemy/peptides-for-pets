The science, simply

What is a copper peptide? The 20-second version.

1. Tiny messengers.

Peptides are tiny pieces of protein. They act like short notes that tell skin cells what to do next.

2. The copper one.

A copper peptide (GHK-Cu) is one of those notes, carrying a tiny bit of copper. People already use it in their own skincare.

3. Straight onto the skin.

Skin & Coat puts it right where it’s needed, every day. No tablets, nothing in their food.

4. Helping skin do its own job.

Skin is always renewing itself and making collagen, which keeps it strong and smooth. Copper peptide supports that natural renewal. Healthy skin grows a healthy coat.

Quick questions

1.What is Skin & Coat?

A daily Spray Gel with copper peptide (GHK-Cu) that supports healthy skin and coat. It’s batch-made in a specialist compounding pharmacy lab in South Africa, for dogs, horses and cats.

2.What is a copper peptide?

A tiny protein messenger that carries a bit of copper. It supports the skin’s own natural renewal. People already use it in their own skincare. Read the full explainer.

3.How long does a bottle last?

It depends on size. A 30 mL bottle lasts a small dog about 50 days and a dog over 30 kg about 21 days. A 50 mL horse bottle lasts an average horse about a month.

4.When will I see a difference?

Every animal is different, so we don’t promise a date. Use it daily for 30 days and take a photo on day 1, 10, 20 and 30, so you can judge for yourself.
