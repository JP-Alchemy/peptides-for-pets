Our Story

Three generations next to the polo field

We grew up next to the polo field. Animals have been part of our lives for three generations.

Dirk Snr, Dirk Jnr and Stephen Stewart played polo together. The Stewart and Uys families go back a long way: polo, and a shared love of animals.

Colin Coetzee is Dirk’s friend from school. He joined the business in 2020 and brings something rare: he knows ingredients, compounds and how they work with the body. He’s an animal lover too.

All four of us come from health and wellness. So we started Peptides for Pets, for the animals we love.

That all four are happy to be named.
